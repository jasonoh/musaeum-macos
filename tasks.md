- [ ] `book-bytes.ts`'s `FORMATS` allowlist is `satisfies BookFormat[]`, which type-checks the literals but does not enforce completeness — a new `BookFormat` member would silently be unservable. Same shape of gap as the sort-key one: it fails as a missing feature, not a type error.

## Deliberately not done (named so they are not re-proposed)

**Post-hydration duplicate report** (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-post-hydration-duplicate-report-design.md`) — no library-wide duplicate scan (`GROUP BY isbn_13` was declined — it would surface the older pairs), no merge action, no stored marker or dismissal, and no per-book naming in the bulk job's count. **Reading this in a dev run:** unkeyed Google fetches settle no ISBN at all, so the check is inert without `GOOGLE_BOOKS_API_KEY` — measured, the first probe attempt reported nothing until the app was launched with the key.

**Field overrides — the lock map** (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-field-overrides-design.md`) — no marker outside the editor (nothing on the card, the detail panel or the conflict queue says a field is held — the obvious next add, and the slice's stated risk 1), no expiry, and no sync between machines _when this shipped_ — the map then lived in `app_config` (`getConfig`/`setConfig`), with no column and no migration. **That is superseded:** portable decisions slice 1 (built 2026-10-11) moved the claims onto the book's record — `books.owned_fields` (migration `007_owned_fields.sql`, with a one-time seed from the old map in `db.ts`) and `owned_fields` in `metadata.json` / `catalog.json` — so they sync between machines on adoption; see the _Portable decisions_ entry above.
